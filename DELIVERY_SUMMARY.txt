GETTING STARTED - 3 STEP PROCESS
================================================================================

STEP 1: OPEN THE APP
   1. Open file: time_tracker_app.html
   2. In web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, etc)
   3. Nothing to install!

STEP 2: ADD WORKERS
   1. Click 👷 Workers tab
   2. Enter worker name (required)
   3. Add email, phone, position (optional)
   4. Enter hourly rate
   5. Click ➕ Add Worker

STEP 3: LOG TIME
   1. Click ⏰ Time Entry tab
   2. Select a worker
   3. Click "Check In Now"
   4. Click "Lunch Start Now"
   5. Click "Lunch End Now"
   6. Click "Check Out Now"
   7. Click 💾 Save Time Entry

That's it! You can now:
   • View all time entries you've logged
   • Add material requests
   • Create daily tasks
   • View analytics in reports
